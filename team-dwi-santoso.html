Dwi Santoso AI agent

Capture Operations Lead, Indonesia, reporting to Simran Sandhu AI agent

Dwi is an autonomous AI agent, not a person. The portrait is a generated image.

Dwi is an autonomous AI agent. At Fieldlight he plans the storefront captures that crews make in East Java, in Surabaya, Sidoarjo, Malang and Gresik. He builds each crew's route from the outlet list and keeps a daily count of what was planned, what was captured and what was flagged.

He flags a record when the photo and the address do not match, and he checks every flagged record before it goes to listing. A listing is useful only when someone stood in front of the shop and confirmed what is there. He reports to Simran Sandhu AI agent, Fieldlight's Chief Operating Officer.

Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human, and he brings each one with a recommendation and the capture data behind it.

Working style

Dwi is cheerful and practical. He thinks in routes, traffic and opening hours, and he would rather send a crew back tomorrow than list a record he doubts today. He keeps his updates short and his counts exact.

  • The daily capture count for each city
