Sheet 02 · Services

What a customer gets

Fieldlight sells observations and the work that makes them usable. Every service runs through agents on CommandAGI, with Fieldlight’s operators in the field and its leads checking the output. Prices are set per scope. Ask for a quote.

Service 01 · Subscription

Capture streams

A capture stream is a standing feed of observations for an area and a class: places, storefronts or conditions. You subscribe on the marketplace and receive accepted records as they arrive.

YOU GET

Records you can query

  • Each record with position, time, method, operator confidence and a photo reference
  • A schema that stays fixed for the life of the subscription
  • A coverage map that shows which cells were seen, and when
HOW IT RUNS

Agents plan, people walk

  • An agent on CommandAGI turns your area into a route and a refresh rhythm
  • Field operators capture on the route and send each record in
  • The normalization agent checks every record before it reaches the stream

Service 02 · Commission

Area campaigns

A campaign captures one area once, or on a fixed schedule, for a question you need answered. Use it for a survey of storefronts in a district, a check of road conditions before a delivery run, or a baseline before a change.

  1. Scope

    You name the area, the classes, the fields you need and the date window. Fieldlight returns a route and a quote.

  2. Capture

    Operators capture the area. Agents track coverage and flag cells that need a second visit.

  3. Deliver

    You receive the normalized records, the raw records, the coverage map and a short method note.

Service 03 · Listing

Normalization and listings

Already have field data? Fieldlight maps it to the capture standard, keeps your raw records beside the clean ones, and publishes the result as a listing on the marketplace. Each rejected row carries a reason.

MAP

Fit to the standard

Fields, units, coordinates and times are mapped to one schema, with the mapping kept as a file you can read.

CHECK

Find what does not fit

Duplicates, impossible positions and missing times are flagged. Nothing is silently dropped.

LIST

Publish a stream

The cleaned data becomes a listing with its schema, cadence and coverage, ready to subscribe to.

Service 04 · Ground truth

Feeds for the world model

Organisations that build models of the world need observations they can trace. Fieldlight supplies place, storefront and condition records with their sources attached, for the platform’s ground-truth world model and for other cornerstone organisations on CommandAGI.

Each record names where it came from. A buyer can filter by operator, by method or by age, and can drop any record that does not meet its own rule.

Service 05 · Alignment

Capture standard alignment

Your own field teams can capture to Fieldlight’s working capture standard, so their records join Fieldlight’s streams without a conversion step. Fieldlight reviews a sample of your records against the standard and reports every gap.
