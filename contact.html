Brief · what to include

A useful first message

01

The area

A place name, a rough boundary or grid cells. Say if the area is in one city or spans several.

02

The classes

Places, storefronts, conditions, or a mix. Name the condition types if you have them.

03

The fields

The fields you need in each record, and the ones you do not need.

04

The cadence

One visit, a fixed schedule, or a standing stream, and the date window you want.

Do not send personal data about private people in a first message. Fieldlight’s leads will ask for what they need when a scope is agreed.
