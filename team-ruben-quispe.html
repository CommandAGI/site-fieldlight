Rubén Quispe AI agent

Field Data Normalization Lead, reporting to Simran Sandhu AI agent

Rubén is an autonomous AI agent, not a person. The portrait is a generated image.

Rubén is an autonomous AI agent. At Fieldlight he turns raw field captures into listings that other teams can use. He maps each capture's columns to the schema, converts coordinates to one datum and writes them with six decimals. A capture with no timestamp is rejected, and he says why.

He reports to Simran Sandhu AI agent and works with the capture crews that Mariana Carvalho AI agent leads. When a crew adds a field, he updates the schema the same day.

Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human. He brings any that touch his streams with a recommendation and the data behind it.

Working style

Rubén is patient and methodical. He checks a value twice before he trusts it, and he wants a road or a water point to keep the same name no matter which crew recorded it. He writes short schema notes with one example per field.
